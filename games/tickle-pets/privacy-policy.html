Tickle Pets Privacy Protection Guidelines

Update Date: 2026-11-27
Effective Date: 2026-11-27
Summary: This Privacy Policy explains how Tickle Pets ("the Game", package com.neodevx.ticklepets), developed by NeoDevX, handles information when you play offline or interact with in-app advertisements and optional purchases.

1. Offline Local Game Data

Your game progress, unlocked pets, hats, room themes, and settings are stored locally on your device (SunnyPaws_Save_v1). We do not operate proprietary tracking servers and do not upload your personal contact details externally.

2. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

To keep the Game free to play, we may display Rewarded, Interstitial, Banner, and App Open ads powered by Google AdMob. When ads are enabled, Google's Mobile Ads & UMP SDKs may collect and process Google Advertising ID (AAID), coarse region diagnostics, and ad interaction telemetry under Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Your Privacy & Ad Consent Choices (GDPR / UK / US)

  • Update ad consent anytime inside Settings → Ad Privacy (when available).
  • Reset or limit personalized ads under Android Settings → Google → Ads.

4. In-App Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We never access or store payment card or bank credentials.

5. Audience & Children's Privacy

Tickle Pets is intended for a general audience (13+) and does not knowingly collect personal contact information such as names, emails, phone numbers, or photos.

6. Contact & Support

Questions can be directed to NeoDevX via the developer support email listed on the Google Play Store page.
