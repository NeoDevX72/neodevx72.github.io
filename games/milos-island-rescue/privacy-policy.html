Milo's Island Rescue Privacy Protection Guidelines

Update Date: November 23, 2026
Effective Date: November 23, 2026
Update tip: summary
This Privacy Policy explains how Milo's Island Rescue ("the Game", package com.milosislandrescue.game), developed by Milo Island Games (NeoDevX), handles information when you play offline or interact with in-app advertisements and optional purchases.

1. Offline Local Game Data

Your game progress, rescued pets, stars, coins, unlocked worlds, Magic Powers inventory, dress-up hats, and audio/haptic settings are stored locally on your device (hatch_merge_pets.json). We do not operate proprietary game servers and do not upload your personal gameplay save files to any external server. You can erase all local progress at any time via Settings → Reset Progress.

2. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

To keep the Game free to play, we display optional Rewarded Video Ads, natural-break Interstitial Ads, Adaptive Banner Ads, and warm-resume App Open Ads powered by Google AdMob. When ads are enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform (UMP) may automatically collect and process:

  • Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID)
  • Coarse location (derived from IP address) and device/network diagnostics
  • Ad impression, click, and fraud-prevention telemetry

This data is processed by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Your Privacy & Ad Consent Choices (GDPR / UK / US)

  • In-App Ad Privacy: You can review or update your ad consent preferences at any time via Settings → Ad Privacy.
  • Device Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device under Settings → Google → Ads.

4. In-App Purchases (No Forced Ads)

Optional purchases (such as "No Forced Ads") are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We never collect, view, or store your credit card, bank, or billing credentials.

5. Audience & Children's Privacy

Milo's Island Rescue is intended for a General Audience (13+). We do not knowingly collect personal contact information (such as names, emails, phone numbers, or photos) from any player.

6. Contact & Support

If you have any questions about these Privacy Protection Guidelines, please contact Milo Island Games via the developer support email listed on our Google Play Store page.
