Studio Privacy Policy

General Privacy Protection Guidelines

Updated: November 23, 2026
Effective: November 23, 2026
Summary: Mobile games published by NeoDevX / Milo Island Games store gameplay progress locally on your device. We do not run proprietary player-tracking servers or sell personal contact details. Advertising and billing, where available, are handled by Google Play Services and Google AdMob.

1. Local Offline Game Data

Gameplay progress, unlocked levels, virtual coins, cosmetic items, and audio/haptic preferences are saved locally on your device in private app storage. Uninstalling an app or clearing app storage removes local progress permanently unless platform cloud backup is enabled in your Android settings.

2. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

Free-to-play titles may display advertisements (such as Rewarded Video, Interstitial, Banner, and App Open ads) powered by Google AdMob. When ads are enabled, Google's Mobile Ads & User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDKs may collect or process:

  • Device advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID)
  • Approximate region/coarse location (derived from IP address) and device/network diagnostics
  • Ad view, click, and fraud-prevention telemetry

Learn how Google processes advertising data in Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Consent & Opt-Out Choices (GDPR / UK / US)

  • In-App Ad Consent: Eligible players can review or update privacy choices anytime under Settings → Ad Privacy inside our games.
  • Device Advertising Controls: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID via Settings → Google → Ads on your device.

4. In-App Purchases

Optional purchases (such as ad-removal upgrades or virtual bundles) are processed strictly through Google Play Billing. We never see, process, or store your payment card or bank credentials.

5. Audience & Children's Privacy

Unless explicitly stated otherwise in a game's dedicated policy page, our titles are designed for a General Audience (13+) and do not knowingly request or collect personal contact information such as names, phone numbers, home addresses, or photos.

6. Per-Game Policies & Support

Please consult each game's dedicated page on our Games & Legal Hub for save-file details or game-specific placements, or contact the developer support email listed on the corresponding Google Play Store page.
